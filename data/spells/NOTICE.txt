SPELL DATABASE SOURCES
======================

data/spells/spells.json and data/spells/icons/*.png are generated by
scripts/fetch-spell-data.py (run scripts/fetch-spell-data.sh to regenerate)
from the World of Warcraft game data served by wago.tools (12.x retail
builds).

The spell names, descriptions and icons are Blizzard Entertainment assets.
Icons are converted from the game's BLP2 textures to 40x40 PNG at build
time. They are bundled into the application for display in the damage meter
tooltips.
